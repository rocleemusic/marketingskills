# Page CRO Audit: Clean Cuts "Work" Category Pages

**Target Page:** cleancuts.com/work/podcasts (and similar category pages)
**Primary Goal:** Transition high-intent visitors from "browsing work" to "starting a project."

---

## 1. Value Proposition Clarity
**Status:** High Artistry, Low Intent.
The pages do an excellent job of showing the *prestige* of the work (logos like Higher Ground, HBO). However, they don't explicitly tell the visitor *why* Clean Cuts was the difference-maker for those specific projects.

## 2. Headline Effectiveness
**Current:** "Podcasts" (Generic category title)
**Recommendation:** Move from a category label to a **Result-Focused Headline.**
*   *Alternative A:* "Award-Winning Narrative Audio for the World's Best Storytellers."
*   *Alternative B:* "Cinematic Sound for High-Prestige Podcast Networks."

## 3. CTA Placement & Hierarchy
**Issue:** The "Start a Project" CTA is often buried in the footer or a small "Contact" button in the nav.
**Recommendation:** Add a **Contextual CTA** at the end of the work grid.
*   *Copy:* "Have a narrative series in development? Let's talk about the soundscape."
*   *Button:* "Start Your Sonic Audit" (High value) vs. "Contact Us" (Low value).

## 4. Trust Signals & Social Proof
**Issue:** You have the logos, but not the **"Why."**
**Recommendation:** Add a "Client Quote" overlay or a small "Success Snippet" for the top 3 items in the grid.
*   *Example:* "For Higher Ground, we built a custom sonic environment that increased listener retention by [X]%." (Or use a qualitative quote if data isn't available).

---

## Quick Wins (Implement Now)
1.  **The "Wedge" Banner:** Add a subtle banner at the bottom of the "Work" pages: *"Talent recording in DC/Baltimore? Book our luxury suites for the day. Lunch is on us."*
2.  **Specific CTAs:** Change "Contact" to "Get a Quote" or "Book a Studio Tour."

## High-Impact Changes (Prioritize)
1.  **Case Study Summaries:** Instead of just a video/audio player, add 2-3 sentences of "Creative Intent" for each project. Explain *what* the challenge was and *how* Clean Cuts solved it.
2.  **Video Testimonials:** If you have client accolades in email, ask if they can record a 15-second "Sound Review" of working with your team.

---

## Copy Alternatives for Hero Sections
*   **Headline:** "Sound Like You Mean It."
*   **Sub-headline:** "Emmy and Grammy® award-winning sound design for agencies, publishers, and networks who refuse to settle for 'good enough'."
*   **CTA:** "Partner with Us" or "Explore the Process."
