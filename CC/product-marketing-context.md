## Product Overview
**One-liner:** Emmy and Grammy® award-winning audio post-production boutique for prestige content creators.
**What it does:** Provides world-class sound design, mixing, ADR, and sonic branding for television, film, podcasts, games, and enterprise brands.

## The "Prestige" Positioning
Clean Cuts differentiates itself through a combination of **Artistry**, **Authority**, and **Reliability**. We aren't just an "audio vendor"; we are a strategic creative partner for high-stakes media.

### Core Differentiators
1.  **The Authority Hook (Prestige)**: We have worked with the world's most demanding creators, including **Higher Ground (The Obamas)**, **Marvel Studios**, and **HBO**. If they trust us, your project is safe.
2.  **The Reliability SLA (The Producer's Peace of Mind)**: 30+ years of technical experience, 10,000+ mixes delivered, and a guaranteed **1-hour response time**.
3.  **The Boutique Experience (Hospitality)**: Luxury recording suites in DC and Baltimore where talent and producers are "taken care of." *Lunches are on us—and they are a legendary part of the experience.*

## Growth Strategy: The "Wedge" Model
We don't lead with a $50k contract. We lead with the **"Recording Space Wedge."**

1.  **The "First Yes"**: When high-profile talent is visiting the DC/Baltimore area, we book them in our luxury suites for the day.
2.  **The Experience**: We provide a world-class recording environment and high-touch hospitality (lunch).
3.  **The Upsell**: Once the trust is built in the room, we pivot to handling the **Full Post-Production & Mixing** contract.
