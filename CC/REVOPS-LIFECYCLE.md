# RevOps Strategy: The Clean Cuts "Prestige" Pipeline

This document defines the lead lifecycle and handoff processes to ensure high-value creative leads are captured, qualified, and closed with zero friction.

---

## 1. Lead Lifecycle Stages

| Stage | Criteria | Action |
|-------|----------|--------|
| **Lead** | Inquiry via website or email (e.g., "Need studio time"). | Automated "Thank You" + 1-hour response notification. |
| **MQL** | Matches ICP (Ad Agency, Publisher, Network) OR High Intent (Talent in town). | **Immediate alert to CEO/Producer.** |
| **SQL** | Discovery call complete; project scope defined. | Draft "One-Pager" or Custom Reel sent to prospect. |
| **Opportunity** | Proposal/Quote sent. | Follow-up every 48 hours until decision. |
| **Customer** | Signed contract or deposit received. | Handoff to Studio Engineer/Lead Creative. |

## 2. The "Prestige" SLA (Service Level Agreement)

Because Clean Cuts differentiates on **Reliability**, our response times must be elite:

*   **Speed-to-Lead**: All inquiries must be acknowledged within **60 minutes** during business hours.
*   **MQL Routing**: Any inquiry from a "Dream Client" (HBO, Audible, Major Agency) must be routed directly to the CEO’s mobile via SMS/Instant Alert.
*   **Proposal Turnaround**: Standard quotes (Studio Rental/Single Mix) must be delivered within **24 hours**. Complex series quotes within **48 hours**.

## 3. Lead Scoring: The "Prestige" Filter

We prioritize leads based on **Fit** (Brand Value) and **Intensity** (Deadlines).

| Signal | Points | Why |
|--------|--------|-----|
| **Brand Recognition** (HBO, Marvel, etc.) | +50 | High-prestige logos drive future business. |
| **"Talent in Town"** | +40 | Our primary high-conversion "Wedge." |
| **Agency Domain** (.agency, .com with "Agency" in name) | +30 | Potential for recurring project flow. |
| **"Tight Turnaround" Mentioned** | +20 | Signals high-stakes need where we excel. |
| **Generic Gmail/Student** | -50 | Low-intent or low-budget "tire kickers." |

## 4. The "Wedge" to "Full-Project" Handoff

The most important RevOps process for Clean Cuts is converting **Studio Rentals** into **Post-Production Contracts**.

1.  **Capture**: When a "Studio Rental" lead comes in, the SDR/Producer checks the client's LinkedIn for upcoming projects.
2.  **The "Lunches" Hook**: During the studio day, the Producer casually introduces the "Post-Production" capabilities while providing the "Boutique Experience."
3.  **The "Magic" Follow-up**: Within 24 hours of the talent leaving, the CEO sends a "Sound Review" of the day's recordings with a note: *"We've already mapped out a creative mix strategy for these files if you want us to handle the post."*

## 5. Recommended Stack (The "Boutique" Stack)
*   **CRM**: HubSpot (Starter) — To track deals and automate the 1-hour "Thank You" emails.
*   **Scheduling**: SavvyCal — To allow agencies to see studio availability in real-time.
*   **Enrichment**: Apollo.io — To identify the "Producers" and "Creative Directors" at target agencies.
