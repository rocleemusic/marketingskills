## Positioning & Messaging

**Primary value proposition**: A dedicated podcast studio that handles everything from story planning to final distribution.

**Target audience**: Marketing departments at major brands and non-profits who want a "TV-quality" podcast.

**Positioning angle**: "Turnkey Podcasting." They position themselves as the "outsourced podcast department" for brands.

**Key messaging themes**:
- **Video Podcasting**: Strong emphasis on the visual side of podcasts (YouTube, Social clips).
- **Strategy First**: Messaging is about *why* to start a podcast, not just how to record it.
- **Convenient DC Studio**: Lean into their physical location for guest interviews.

## Strengths & Weaknesses

### Strengths
- **Niche Focus**: They only do podcasts, so they feel like "experts" in that specific format.
- **Visual Assets**: Excellent at creating the "viral clips" that marketing teams want.
- **Turnkey**: They lower the barrier to entry for brands that don't know where to start.

### Weaknesses
- **Audio Depth**: Lacks the deep "Sonic Branding" and "Cinematic" audio heritage of Clean Cuts.
- **Boutique Scale**: May not have the depth for massive narrative series (HBO style) vs. talk-show podcasts.
