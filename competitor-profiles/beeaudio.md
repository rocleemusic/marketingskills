## Strengths & Weaknesses

### Strengths
- **Efficiency**: They are the "low cost / high volume" choice for publishers.
- **Specific Expertise**: They know the nuances of ACX/Audible specs better than anyone.
- **Narrator Network**: Incredible breadth of voice options.

### Weaknesses
- **"The Factory" Feel**: For high-end "prestige" titles or celebrity memoirs, they might feel too industrial.
- **Less "High-End Sound Design"**: Their focus is on clarity and fidelity, not cinematic soundscapes.
