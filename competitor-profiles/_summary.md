## Key Strategic Takeaways
1.  **The "Prestige" Gap**: While Henninger wins on *scale* and Bee Audio wins on *volume*, Clean Cuts owns the *prestige* segment. Neither of these competitors can effectively use the "Worked with the Obamas" or "Grammy-winning" hooks with the same weight.
2.  **The "Strategy" Threat**: Podville is winning clients by offering "Podcast Strategy" and "Social Clips." This is a gap for Clean Cuts—adding a "Strategy & Social Growth" layer to our high-end audio could neutralize this threat.
3.  **The "Recording Space" Wedge**: Both Henninger and Podville use their DC location as a hook. Clean Cuts should double down on the **"Luxury Studio / Taken Care Of"** messaging to win the "Talent in Town" segments.

## Gaps and Opportunities
*   **The "Celebrity Audiobook" Wedge**: While Bee Audio handles thousands of books, they lack the "prestige" feel for A-list celebrity memoirs. Clean Cuts can own this niche by positioning our rooms as the luxury standard for DC/Baltimore-based VIPs.
*   **Podcast "Soundscapes"**: Most branded podcasts (Podville) sound like talk shows. Clean Cuts can win by offering "Cinematic Soundscapes" that make a branded podcast sound like a Netflix series.
